Is there a tick on you?
Do a tick check!
Here’s where to look:
Inside and behind the
ears
Along your hairline
Back of your neck
Armpits
Groin (where your legs
join your body)
Legs
Behind the knees
Between your toes
Ticks are tiny, so look for
new “freckles.”
