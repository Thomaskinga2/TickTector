Protect yourself and family from tick bites and disease by
using repellent such as DEET, tucking your pants into socks,
using Permethrin treated clothing, avoiding tick prone areas
and doing tick checks after outdoor activities.
Up to 30% of people DO NOT get a rash with Lyme disease
and there may NOT BE A RASH with the other tick-borne
diseases. Check with your practitioner if you have had a tick
bite, the time the tick is attached and able to transmit disease
varies.

Ticks are sensitive to dryness,
hot water does not bother
them. So take clothes off
when you come inside, put
them in the dryer first! Put on
high heat for greater than 15
minutes. If you put your
clothes in the hamper or on a
chair the ticks will crawl
away.
Ticks start low and crawl up.
They go to dark places so
check all out of the way
spots for example behind
ears, groin area, and under
arms. Ticks do not come off
in the shower and hot water
does not bother them, but it
is a good place to do a tick
check.
Groom your pets after they are
outside to protect your pet and
prevent ticks in your home.
Dogs and cats walk through
higher grass and places where
ticks are and usually encounter
more ticks then people do.
